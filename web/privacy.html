Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

This policy explains what Storm Exchange (run by the Storm Exchange team, contact stormexchangefn@gmail.com) collects, why, and your choices. We keep it to what the game needs.

What we collect

  • Account: your username, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we never see them.
  • Discord (only if you link it): your Discord user ID, username and avatar, received through Discord's own login. With your permission Discord also lets our bot add you to the Storm Exchange Discord server and give you roles there.
  • Game activity: your trades, positions, gold bars, net worth, and when you accepted the Terms.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers record standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the site. Your browser stores a few preferences (such as your chosen region filter) and your login session on your device.

We don't sell your data, don't show ads, and don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

What is public

Storm Exchange is a public game. Your username, your Discord name and avatar (if linked), and your trades, positions and net worth can be seen by anyone on the site, including on the leaderboard. Your email address is never shown.

Why we use it

  • to run your account and the game (performing our agreement with you);
  • to confirm your email and reset your password;
  • to verify Discord accounts and manage Discord roles;
  • to prevent cheating and keep the site secure (our legitimate interests).

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database and account login.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Discord: account linking, server membership and roles (if you link Discord).
  • Our email delivery provider: sends sign-up and password emails on our behalf.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: the bot check on the log-in and sign-up forms. It looks at your IP address and browser signals to tell people from bots, and doesn't set tracking cookies.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver the site's fonts and a code library, so they receive your IP address when pages load.

Some of these providers are in the United States, so your data may be processed there.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it exists. Past seasons' results may be kept for the season history. If you delete your account we remove your personal data, except where we must keep something for legal reasons.

Your choices and rights

  • You can unlink Discord at any time from your account.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time: open your account (top right) and choose "Delete my account". This removes your profile, portfolio, positions and trade history.
  • You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data by emailing stormexchangefn@gmail.com.
  • If you're in the EU, UK or a US state with privacy laws, you also have the rights those laws give you, including complaining to your data protection authority.

Children

Storm Exchange is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email stormexchangefn@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

Changes

We'll post updates here and change the date above. For important changes we'll ask you to review them on the site.
